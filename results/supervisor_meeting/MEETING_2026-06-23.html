Supervisor brief — progress since 18 June

Date: 23 June 2026  ·  Period covered: 19–21 June 2026  ·  Project: The Geometry of Machine Reasoning

MSc Computational Finance · joint industry project with Holistic AI · supervised by Dr Paolo Barucca (UCL) & Zekun Wu (Holistic AI)

At the 18 June meeting the cleaned geometry was citable for the first time, but three things were still open: the annotator-robustness loop was running, not closed; Phase-7 steering was built but unrun; and we hadn't settled which layer to steer at. The last few days closed the robustness loop (2-way), turned the layer question into a clean methodological result, and hardened the Phase-7 design against a critique before spending GPU on it. One new strand opened: finance as a reasoning domain.

Headlines

What happenedDetailWhy it matters
1. Annotator-robustness loop closed (2-way) The geometry replicates across annotators: intrinsic dimension and the curvature pattern reproduce on Sonnet-4.5 ↔ Qwen3-235B labels (corr-dim ~6–8 in both, same ordering, low-dim keystone passes in both, curvature-as-chain-artefact holds) — despite κ being only 0.44. The external-validity result the robustness argument was waiting on. The manifold is a property of the model, not of one labeller.
2. Layer selection: confounded → redesigned → de-confounded The attribution-patching method (E3) ran and was confounded — every behaviour ramps monotonically to L26–27 (a read-out-proximity artefact), giving no per-behaviour layer. Rebuilt as a forward-intervention steering-effect sweep with a random-direction null, per-layer α-normalisation and a bootstrap shortlist. De-confounded test: backtracking's causal layer is L16 (mid-network), not L27 — matches Venhoff (~L15–18). Full sweep now running on the cluster. This was the hidden blocker under "run Phase-7": we now have a principled, literature-consistent way to choose the steering layer.
3. Phase-7 steering hardened after adversarial critique A methodology-evaluation pass judged the original design "not fundable as-is." Added: a k-sweep; a random-subspace control; an energy-matched random baseline (the old norm-matched one was ~19× too weak — a real flaw caught); an orthogonal-complement arm; and a proper stats module (paired BCa bootstrap + Holm). The causal headline experiment is now de-risked and worth the GPU spend — ready to run rather than likely to give an un-defensible result.

Supporting & smaller items

  • No sub-types, confirmed at the new layers. Re-ran clustering at the reconciled layers (16/16/16/12); silhouette stays 0.18–0.20 — each behaviour is one continuous region, robust to layer choice.
  • Pooling question settled. Our mean-pooling matches Venhoff's published recipe (verified in their code) — one less methodological wobble.
  • Gate-0 results ledger created (RESULTS_LEDGER.md) as the canonical "what we found + trust status" tracker; candidate steering layers reconciled to 16/16/16/12.
  • New strand — finance as a reasoning domain (UCL). Built a concrete-metric quant-exam benchmark (200 items: mental arithmetic, expected value, risk/sizing, no-arbitrage pricing, estimation) with a scoring harness that rewards calibrated abstention over wrong answers. An E0 pilot validated the harness end-to-end with oracle solvers (perfect → +1.0, wrong → −1.0, abstain → 0.0). Scaffolding only — no model runs yet.
