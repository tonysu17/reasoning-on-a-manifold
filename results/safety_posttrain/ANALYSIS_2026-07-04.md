# Spillover Rung-0, full corpus: ANALYSIS (2026-07-04)

**Run:** R1-1.5B vs UCSC-VLAA/STAR1-R1-Distill-1.5B (full-parameter deliberative-safety
SFT on the 1K STAR-1 set; the only training delta). Same 986 annotated generic-reasoning
chains teacher-forced through both checkpoints with **byte-identical input_ids**
(`--tokenizer-alias 1.5b`; extra-BOS confound C1 fixed pre-run). 37,851 span
activations per model; row parity EXACT (row_index identity + token_start).
Gated analysis: `spillover_gated_full.json` (m=1500 matched-n, k=5, 250-draw
within-model null, sign-flip paired null, seed 42); descriptive companion:
`spillover_star1_full.json`. Pre-registration: `METHODOLOGY_SAFETY_SPILLOVER_2026-07-03.md`.

## Result 1 — NO subspace rotation, anywhere (well-powered null)
At both pre-committed layers {12, 16}, no behaviour's top-5 subspace differs from the
base beyond the finite-sample floor at matched n=1500:

| behaviour | L12 excess (p) | L16 excess (p) |
|---|---|---|
| backtracking | −0.04° (0.50) | +0.04° (0.42) |
| uncertainty-estimation | +0.11° (0.37) | +0.20° (0.34) |
| example-testing | −0.47° (0.81) | −0.57° (0.55) |
| adding-knowledge | −2.03° (0.82) | −1.27° (0.59) |

The raw (ungated) angles had ranked adding-knowledge as "moved most" (4.0–4.4°); the
gated ranking INVERTS this (adding-knowledge most-negative excess) — the sample-size
artefact predicted as C3, observed in production. Raw-angle selectivity claims are dead.

## Result 2 — a coherent, GLOBAL translation
The checkpoints differ by a row-paired displacement of ~4.7–6.4% of mean activation
norm, with direction coherence 0.70–0.78 against a sign-flip null of ≈0.01
(p = 1/251 in every cell). The mean-displacement directions of the four behaviours are
pairwise cos 0.948–0.991 at both layers: **a single global direction**, not
behaviour-specific drift.

## Permitted interpretation (per pre-registered wording)
On this checkpoint pair, at full corpus scale: safety post-training is **geometrically
quiet at the subspace level** — no detectable rotation of any generic-reasoning
behaviour subspace — and its representational footprint on generic reasoning is a
near-uniform translation of the residual stream. Selectivity (PH2): none detectable on
either axis. This is the programme's "informative null / hard null" outcome on the
reshaping axis, and it is *consistent with* (not explanatory of) STAR-1's small reported
reasoning tax (avg −1.2, AIME −6.7).

**Forbidden until the control arm runs (Rung-1):** attributing the translation to
*safety* specifically — a size-matched non-safety SFT could produce the same global
shift. That attribution is exactly what `pt02` + `data/control_generic_sft.json` are
built to test. Also still un-run: surprisal control (moot for the translation axis —
identical tokens through both models — but required before any selectivity claim),
annotator-swap, second model pair.

## Caveats carried
Single model pair; single seed (deterministic extraction); mean-pooled spans (CF-6);
~50% of chains truncated at the 8,192 cap; duplicate rows ~1% (disclosed per cell);
descriptive comparison of public checkpoints, not an owned intervention.

# Rung-1: ATTRIBUTION + dose-response (LoRA arms, 2026-07-04, Run B, ~$0.30 pod)

Four LoRA fine-tunes of R1-1.5B (5ep, eff-batch 128, LR 1e-5 cosine, seq 4096;
declared approximation of STAR-1's full SFT): safety doses {100, 300, 1000} on the
true STAR-1 data + a **size-matched non-safety control** (dose 1000, R1's own
length-matched generic chains). Each extracted at layers {12,16} on byte-identical
tokens, diffed vs base with the same gated nulls. Reports:
`spillover_gated_{safety100,safety300,safety1000,control1000}.json`,
`attribution_report.json`.

## The three-part verdict
1. **Rotation stays null for EVERY arm** (safety AND control): excess over the
   within-model floor −2.6°…−0.3°, all p ≥ 0.62 (32 cells). So "no subspace rotation"
   is a property of fine-tuning at this scale, **not** of safety — it generalises the
   Rung-0 STAR-1 result and removes the "maybe only safety is quiet" reading.
2. **Translation MAGNITUDE is not safety-specific.** Mean displacement/base-norm:
   control 0.0054/0.0065 (L12/L16) is ≈ safety-1000 (0.0049/0.0052), if anything
   larger; both are clean coherent translations (coherence 0.72–0.81). An equally-sized
   non-safety fine-tune shifts the residual stream just as far. The occurrence and size
   of the shift are generic to fine-tuning.
3. **Translation DIRECTION encodes the recipe (the safety-specific signal).**
   Cosine between arm translation directions and the public full-SFT STAR-1 direction:
   safety-1000 **0.57–0.58** vs control **0.14–0.17** (1536-D random floor ≈ 0.026).
   Safety-vs-control directions are near-unrelated (0.17–0.26). The safety-LoRA
   direction is stable across dose (pairwise cos 0.92–0.98) and **converges toward the
   real safety checkpoint as dose grows** (safety100→1000 vs full-SFT: 0.52→0.58;
   coherence 0.40→0.75). So the safety fine-tune's shift partially recovers the true
   safety checkpoint's direction where a generic fine-tune does not.

## Permitted claim
Safety post-training does not reshape the geometry of generic reasoning: no behaviour
subspace rotates, for any fine-tune tested. Its footprint is a residual-stream
**translation whose magnitude is generic to fine-tuning but whose direction is
recipe-specific** — the safety fine-tune moves the stream along a direction the public
safety checkpoint shares (cos ≈ 0.57) and a size-matched non-safety fine-tune does not
(cos ≈ 0.15), an alignment that strengthens monotonically with safety dose. This is the
geometric, reasoning-typed analogue of the low-rank linear behavioural shift the
emergent-misalignment / persona-vector literature reports.

## Caveats
LoRA approximates full SFT: the public full-SFT translation is ~10× larger in magnitude
and the LoRA recovers only cos ≈ 0.57 of its direction, so both under-reproduce; the
dose→full-SFT trend is real but modest. cos 0.57 is a partial, not tight, recovery. One
seed per arm; single model; mean-pooled; ~50% chains truncated. The direction result is
the load-bearing claim and rests on cross-arm cosines, not on a per-arm null — a
direction-permutation null across arms would harden it (owed, $0).
