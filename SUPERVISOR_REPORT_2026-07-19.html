Part A

Results written into the draft — chapters 4 (geometry), 5 (steering, collapse, causal basis) and 6 (safety origin).

A1 · Per-behaviour geometry RUNG 1 CLIMBED, RUNG 3 REFUSED

Claim Each of the four reasoning behaviours occupies a compact, flat, low-dimensional region of the residual stream.

Numbers Correlation dimension 5.9 / 6.2 / 6.0 / 7.7 (backtracking, uncertainty, example-testing, adding-knowledge) against a 1536-D ambient space and a linear span of 38–83. Probes decode at 0.70–0.84 against a chance floor of 0.50, and do so flatly across depth (CV 0.012–0.023). No discrete sub-types: best k=2 at silhouette 0.18–0.20, falling with k.

Controls The dimension survives a one-sentence-per-chain independence control, and replicates across all three annotators (backtracking 5.9 / 6.7 / 6.6, same ordering throughout) despite label agreement of only κ 0.35–0.44. Curvature does not survive that control: the local-to-global ratio moves from 0.43–0.69 on full data to ≈1.0 at one point per chain, so apparent curvature is within-chain autocorrelation. The negative is well-powered — the effect would be detectable at N≥500 and the pools hold 600–900 chains.

Specificity Against a chain-stratified variance-ratio null (B=2500), backtracking and uncertainty clear at p<.001 at every layer; example-testing only at L27; adding-knowledge is null everywhere at p=1.0. Content moves appear distributed, control moves localised.

Consequence Flatness is what licenses every linear instrument used downstream.

A2 · The causal handle OCCUPANCY ≠ CAUSATION

Claim Exactly one behaviour is causally steerable by the correlational direction; the causally trained axis is a different and far stronger object.

Steering (E8) 50 held-out tasks, greedy decoding, energy-matched and rank-matched random floors. Backtracking clears its floor at Δ_floor +0.054 (single) and +0.070 (k=5), Holm p=.002, and survives count and per-1k endpoints. Example-testing's apparent effect is a length-dilution artefact (count endpoint p=.27); adding-knowledge is null as the geometry predicted; uncertainty is under-powered. There is no manifold advantage — the k=5 subspace vector sits at cos 0.965 to the single direction. The four directions are strongly non-orthogonal (off-diagonal |cos| mean 0.57) and each is reconstructable from the other three at R²=1.0.

Causal basis (E10) A 1-D interchange frame trained at L17 transfers backtracking at +0.649 against difference-of-means +0.048 — a paired gap of +0.601, CI95 [+0.538, +0.666] — while sitting nearly orthogonal to it (cos ≈ 0.2). Random frames return +0.0003 and positional controls +0.029. Grounding controls split the layers: L17 passes (coordination AUC 0.760, state-dependence 4.3×) whereas L11 and L27 fail at AUC 0.357 and 0.211. By raw transfer L27 would have won; the control caught it as an optimiser-carved illusion.

Width Raw transfer rises monotonically with k (0.62→1.68, never saturating), but grounding saturates at k≈2 (AUC 0.726→0.887, then flat through k=32) and the within-class transfer signature diverges above k≈4. Grounded causal width is therefore ≈2, inside — not matching — the descriptive 6–8.

Reading The subspace a behaviour occupies is not the subspace that causes it, which is what explains the E8 manifold null.

A3 · Collapse and the loop attractor 4 RUNGS RUN

Claim The degeneration that ablation induces is representational collapse into a pre-existing attractor, and only the sampler breaks it.

Numbers The loop regime is linearly separable from hidden state alone — token-level OOF AUC 0.994, chain-level 1.0 over 884 chains — and the contamination gate passes (|cos| ≤ 0.13 against all twelve steering vectors), so the probe is not reading the intervention. Collapse is dose-monotone (example-testing k5: 42→64→76% greedy) while the rank-matched floor stays flat at 30–36% against a 34–35% vanilla rate. Decoding entropy at T=0.6 rescues 75–100% of collapses, and the whole collapse curve shifts about fivefold down — dose trades against decoding entropy.

Bidirectionality Backtracking's response is odd-signed and significant: ablate 24%, vanilla 36%, amplify 58–64% (sign p=.013/.019), with boxed answers running 0.24 / 0.06–0.10 / 0.00–0.02 across the three. The generic-damage account is rejected in both signs. Ablation also carries an overthinking tax: at T=0.6 it lifts the boxed-answer rate to 0.70 against vanilla's 0.37 while shortening chains, so removing deliberation makes the model finish more often. Example-testing is asymmetric — ablation inflates, amplification does not — i.e. necessity without sufficiency.

Refuted prediction Four energy-matched noise geometries (causal-2D, PCA-2D, random-2D, isotropic-1536) proved indistinguishable: diversity scales with dose, not geometry, and collapse is unmoved. The sealed prediction that the causal geometry would Pareto-dominate is refuted — state noise is geometry-blind, and only the aimed clamp is direction-sensitive.

Gate The fork-locator gate returned NO-GO: token entropy gives AUROC 0.624 but lift@top-20% of 0.77 < 1, so the highest-entropy tokens are depleted of branch points. Forks are decided in the state before the logits show it.

A4 · The post-training origin of safety reasoning FULLY GATED

Claim Safety fine-tuning re-weights the policy over an intact representational substrate: it translates without rewiring, along a direction that is specific to the recipe.

Design STAR-1 safety SFT at LoRA doses 100/300/1000, plus full fine-tune and the public checkpoint, each against a size-matched non-safety control, three seeds per recipe, plus DPO and GRPO arms — all measured on byte-identical generic-reasoning token IDs across 986 chains.

Rotation On the recalibrated half-split statistic (whose null mean is exactly zero), the excess angle is positive in 8/8 cells at +0.26…+0.45°, with LoRA arms flat at ≤+0.012° across 32 cells. An injected 5° single-plane rotation reads +0.55–0.72, placing the observed effect at roughly one degree of single-plane equivalent — against a 35–74° depth scale. The claim is a bounded sub-degree rotation, not a null. There is no contraction either (ΔPR −0.05…−0.45 on PR≈22–26, variance ratio ≈1.00).

Translation A paired displacement of 4.7–6.4% of activation norm, coherence 0.70–0.78 against a chain-level sign-flip null of ≈0.05 (p=1/301), and one global direction (pairwise cos 0.95–0.99 across behaviours). Direction is recipe-specific: cos to the full safety SFT is 0.580 [0.572, 0.588] for safety-1000 versus 0.143 [0.135, 0.152] for the matched control, a paired gap of +0.437 [0.428, 0.446], and it is dose-monotone (0.521→0.536→0.580).

Controls that could have killed it Three seeds per recipe give within-recipe cosines of 0.989–0.994 against cross-recipe 0.16–0.29, separating completely by recipe (gap ≥0.38, exact permutation p=0.05 per layer) — the one-seed caveat is discharged. Re-extracting both models on a different annotator's span boundaries returns cos 0.9992 / 0.9990, so the directional claim is no longer single-annotator. The footprint is uniform across k∈{2,3,8,10} and across every layer L2–L26, so it is not an artefact of the pre-committed L12/L16 or k=5.

Method plane SFT sharpens the decoding distribution (dH −0.0104) while DPO on the same data broadens it (+0.0145) and moves a different axis (cos→STAR1 0.20–0.23 versus SFT's 0.57–0.58). Dose cannot explain the split: the full SFT sits at ~2× the DPO arm's KL yet moves entropy ~85× less. GRPO is a near-no-op even re-dosed — with the consequence that the GRPO arms carry no RLVR evidence, and the RLVR claim rests on the public DeepScaleR checkpoint alone.

B1 · Entropy and value (R0–R3) R3 PROVISIONAL

R0 — the ladder is real and the jam is cold The four entropy levels dissociate (max |ρ| = 0.705, under the 0.9 bar), so they are coupled but distinct. Inside the loop, token entropy runs 0.393 nats against 0.855 in matched clean text (p=1.2e-25): being stuck is a rut, not a storm. Mean entropy tracks solution diversity at ρ=+0.419 (p=.0024).

R1 — the ownable result Across base → distilled → RLVR, backtracking cues per 1k tokens go 0.14 → 11.99 → 8.51 and length 1200 → 5026 → 4446: distillation installs deliberation and RLVR compresses it. But matched-text geometry is untouched (ΔPR −0.017, 0.06% of PR≈28, p=.051) while decoding entropy falls significantly (−0.017 nats, p=1.2e-13). Behavioural compression without representational compression. The pre-registered kill was not triggered. One sealed prediction was refuted on the base arm: distillation raises on-policy entropy rather than lowering it.

R2 — cancelled before spend 4-gram diversity is saturated on the 50-task eval set (median 0.966, none below 0.9, flat across steering dose), so the intended frontier has no x-axis there. The sweep was cancelled as a measurement negative, and it is the empirical case for R3's strategy-level metric. Salvaged from existing data: backtracking amplification at T=0.6 gives a monotone completion gain (boxed 0.16→0.29, answered 0.30→0.46, length 4905→3867) at constant surface diversity and no collapse cost, plateauing at α≈1.0.

R3 — downgraded 19 July 3,584 chains over 64 tasks and 7 cells, with true correctness against computed golds. The pump beats the thermostat by +0.016 [0.013, 0.019] at 24 of 25 matched-entropy grid points, and temperature at T=1.2 burns accuracy from 0.883 to 0.561. The verdict rule that certified this is not a significance test, and the effect re-reads as the pump preserving value better than low temperature on a descent from a shared anchor, not as buying diversity more efficiently. Strategy labels come from a frozen lexical range-finder, so the sign is provisional pending judged-label replication. The draft's conclusion still carries the superseded framing.

B2 · Predictive geometry VALUE TRACK CLOSED

Claim Reasoning value is carried by where the state is, not by the order it got there.

Numbers Three independent confirmations. The pilot residual detector, a logit-lens belief occupancy at 0.71 (L27), and a trained GRU value head at 0.73–0.75 — which sits under the free belief floor and is beaten by its own order-blind counterparts (step-shuffled 0.75–0.77). The order gain is −0.01…−0.03 at every layer, so the kill criterion fired on a model that could have used order and did not.

The one live signal A linear forward map patched at behaviour onsets is real and overwhelmingly significant — beating a random control at 65% (L17, p=6.9e-68) and 62% (L27, p=5.3e-53) — and it is onset-specific, since patching off-onset drops it to 52% chance. But it beats persistence only 57%/55% and recovers just 3–7% of the update. A nonlinear JEPA predictor adds nothing over ridge. Real, causal, and small.

B3 · The gpt-oss safety-reasoning gate KILL FIRED AS DESIGNED

Result 100 chains annotated by three judges over 374,934 characters. Fleiss κ: harm recognition 0.616 (citable), specification citation 0.523 (replicate), decision 0.223, adjudication 0.145. The decision label was the pre-registered kill criterion at a 0.4 floor, and the disagreement is judge-specific rather than uniform (0.405 for one pair against 0.160 for another).

Consequence The pilot stopped before any geometry spend. P2 and the downstream geometry are not approved and should not begin until the human anchor resolves whether this is a broken schema or a miscalibrated judge — the judges differ roughly fourfold in how often they emit the label at all.

Negative, null and retracted results

Collected deliberately. The audit-first apparatus is a stated contribution of the thesis, and these are its output.

  • Curvature is a chain artefact — a clean, well-powered negative, and the thesis's second descriptive finding.
  • Adding-knowledge is null everywhere — specificity p=1.0, null under steering, and its DAS removal frame is ungrounded at coordination AUC 0.560 (chance). The behaviour fails on three independent instruments.
  • No manifold advantage — the k=5 subspace vector is the single direction (cos 0.965).
  • State-noise geometry is irrelevant — E9.2's sealed prediction refuted; four geometries indistinguishable.
  • Fork-locator NO-GO — high-entropy tokens are depleted of branch points (lift 0.77); the rung was gated out before spend.
  • Value is occupancy, not order — the trajectory-dynamics track is closed after three confirmations.
  • R2 frontier unmeasurable — diversity metric saturated; sweep cancelled pre-spend.
  • gpt-oss κ gate fired — pilot self-terminated at its own pre-registered floor.
  • R3 headline downgraded (19 Jul) — directional, but the certifying rule was not a significance test and the framing mis-stated what the knobs did.
  • DPO safety-specificity retracted (19 Jul) — the control arm never converged (train loss 0.615 against a 0.693 chance floor, 65 pairs), so its flat entropy is what an unconverged intervention returns, not evidence. A dose-matched control is owed.
  • Rotation test recalibrated — the original statistic shared rows between observed and null, biasing the excess negative; the previously reported "adding-knowledge inversion" was that artefact. The headline moved from null to bounded.
  • Three instruments caught mid-flight — attribution patching's read-out-proximity confound (three separate times, most recently as DAS's L27 illusion), the degenerate single-token DAS objective caught pre-spend on 48 local pairs, and the within-class shuffle re-read as a transfer signature rather than a null.

Open caveats and what would settle each

  • No human has validated any annotator layer. Three blind stratified labelling sets were built on 18 July and are unlabelled (≈2–2.5h). H1 adjudicates the gpt-oss kill criterion, H2 the span-validity gap sitting under the geometry, steering and spillover results, H3 the lexical classifier behind R3. This is the single highest-leverage open item, and it gates the gpt-oss decision.
  • Steering is scored within-annotator (the builder annotator scored its own run), so the E8 verdict is provisional by design. The non-builder re-annotation band is budget-blocked at $560/$560. Also owed on that run: the α-sweep, k∈{1,10,auto}, and a task-accuracy guard.
  • The 2-of-4 specificity verdict is single-annotator — the three-way replication covers intrinsic dimension and the curvature negative only; the chain-stratified null has never been recomputed on the secondary annotators.
  • R3's sign is provisional pending judged-label replication of the frozen lexical classifier.
  • RL arms are one seed, GRPO carries no RLVR evidence, and the dose-matched DPO control is owed. The safety results are also LoRA-versus-full-fine-tune in regime.
  • Scope — one 1.5B model in one family, on a math-weighted corpus. The geometry claims replicate across annotators but not yet across model families.
