# The Predictive Geometry of Reasoning

*A side project of "The Geometry of Reasoning" thesis. Branch: `predictive-geometry-of-reasoning`.*

## 1. One sentence

Train a model to predict the **next reasoning step of a chain-of-thought in latent (embedding) space**, and test whether the **geometry of where that predictor succeeds and fails** is a signature of correct reasoning — moving the thesis's claim that "reasoning is a geometric process" from a *descriptive* statement about static representations to a *dynamical, predictive* one about how reasoning unfolds in time.

## 2. Why this, why now

The thesis (Movement 1, *Structure*) shows that per-behaviour reasoning representations occupy low-dimensional, structured subspaces. But it deliberately leaves a door open:

- **ch07** tests per-behaviour *curvature*, finds it confounded by within-chain autocorrelation, and **relocates it "as an open question, to the trajectory"** — "if curvature lives anywhere in this data it lives in that trajectory… left to future work."
- **ch02** commits to **"locus-in-process"**: "it is the unfolding *trajectory* and not any static activation point that bears the reasoning… the natural future experiments are trajectory-based."

JEPA (Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture — LeCun's program; I-JEPA, V-JEPA 2, LeJEPA) is precisely a machine for **predicting future states in a learned latent space while discarding unpredictable detail**. It is therefore the natural apparatus for the trajectory question the thesis promised but did not build. This project is that apparatus.

## 3. The core question

> **Is reasoning a *predictable* geometric process, and does its *predictability structure* distinguish good reasoning from bad?**

Concretely: given the embedding of reasoning steps `x_1 … x_t`, predict `x_{t+1}`. Study the **residual** `r_t = x_{t+1} − f(x_≤t)` — the part of the next step the predictor could *not* anticipate. The bet is that where the predictor breaks (sharp residuals) localizes the *branch points / backtracks* of reasoning, and that the structure of those breaks separates correct from incorrect chains.

## 4. Hypotheses

| # | Hypothesis | Primary metric | Must beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| **H1** (keystone, functional) | Trajectory **predictability** (residual magnitude / growth / direction-churn) predicts chain-level **correctness** | chain-grouped ROC-AUC | token-NLL, raw-curvature, length+difficulty; and both nulls |
| **H2** (representational) | A *learned* predictor recovers correctness/behaviour structure that raw hidden states and a random projection do not | probe AUC / decoding | raw `x`, random projection, untrained predictor |
| **H3** (geometric) | Residual spikes localize geometrically special points (high curvature / subspace transitions = *backtracking*); correct vs incorrect trajectories differ here after difficulty control | curvature@residual-peaks; matched-pair Δ | within-chain shuffle null |
| **H4** (causal, apex — *confirmation, not novelty*) | Steering along the **predicted next-step direction** changes reasoning more than a random direction | behaviour/correctness shift under steering | random-direction & mean-direction controls |

**Null discipline (non-negotiable).** Every positive claim must survive (a) **chain-stratified label permutation** (does the signal exceed chance given chain composition?) and (b) **within-chain step-order shuffle** (does it need the genuine temporal order, or is it a static-cloud artifact?). A clean negative that beats neither is itself a reportable result.

## 5. What is genuinely novel (and what is not)

- **Novel (our atom), NARROWED (2026-06-15 lit pass):** a forward predictor over the **behaviour-segmented reasoning _steps_** whose **residual _geometry over time_** — *where* it spikes — localizes branch/backtrack points and classifies chains under chain-grouped nulls. We do **NOT** claim to be first to relate predictor unpredictability to correctness — that claim is retired: **PHi (arXiv:2503.13431)** already does this at the _per-token_ residual-stream level, and **SSP (2604.18464)** trains a next-_step_ predictor but reads _smoothness_ (AUC≈0.5). The defensible wedge: step/behaviour segmentation + residual _geometry_ (not a per-token scalar) + explicit branch-point localization, all under the chain-stratified + step-shuffle nulls.
- **Already taken — do not claim:** predictor-unpredictability↔correctness at the per-token level (PHi 2503.13431); "low-dimensional curved reasoning manifold" (arXiv:2510.09782 ≈ our thesis title; SSP 2604.18464; phase-space 2410.04415); "predict next step in latent space" (COCONUT 2412.06769, generative; LLM-JEPA 2509.14252, paired-view); "steer along a predicted/ideal next-state direction" (ASM, ICLR 2026).
- **Our wedge against the nearest neighbours:** SSP (2604.18464) reports that trajectory *smoothness* does **not** encode correctness (AUC≈0.5) and explicitly declines to build a latent-prediction feedback loop; 2604.05655 gets AUC 0.87 but from a *static* activation-difference probe. Our quantity — a *learned predictor's residual geometry* — is a **third thing**, and it directly targets the open question SSP left negative.
- **LRS (arXiv 2606.00726, read 2026-07-06):** value-gradient steering over SAE latents — trains a latent reward model on final-answer correctness, follows its gradient at inference, gated on reward+confidence. The *causal value-track* sibling. Not pre-empted by it: LRS has no causal validation of its directions (uninspected gradient field), no energy/count-matched intervention floors, test-set-tuned knobs, no val checkpoint. Our wedge vs LRS = **mechanism, not benchmark gains**: triangulation of the value gradient against causally grounded frames (E10.1 DAS), intervention-validated precursors (E9.1b dose), and the WHEN-vs-WHERE factorization its state-dependent direction structurally cannot run (§11).

## 6. Method — the escalation ladder

Each rung is gated: it is pursued only if it beats the previous rung **and** both nulls.

- **Rung 0 — predictor-free.** Raw-trajectory Frenet curvature (existing `src/cbs/trajectory.py`) on difficulty-matched correct/incorrect chains. Cheap; tells us if *any* trajectory geometry separates correctness before we build anything.
- **Rung 1 — linear, no anti-collapse.** Ridge predictor of the **displacement** `x_{t+1}−x_t` (a residual/skip connection, so the residual measures the *unpredictable* part and the predictor can only improve on persistence). Residual geometry has **no** anti-collapse regulariser, so reading geometry off it is not circular. *(Built and tested.)*
- **Rung 2 — small JEPA.** A compact learned predictor (causal MLP/transformer over step embeddings, or the LLM-JEPA `[PRED]`-token trick) with **SIGReg** (LeJEPA) or **Barlow-Twins** anti-collapse. Pursued only to *beat* Rung 1; only *relative/functional* claims (never absolute intrinsic-dim/isotropy of the regularised latent).
- ~~**Rung 3 — apex.** Goal-conditioned latent rollout + CEM planning, and **causal steering** along the predicted direction via `src/steered_inference.SteeredModel`.~~ **SUPERSEDED 2026-07-06** by the adopted reframe (§11): the causal closed loop is now **R6** (precursor-gated ablation of a *validated* direction — grounded where CEM-along-predicted-direction was speculative and ASM-pre-empted); latent rollout survives only inside **R7** (action-conditioned forward model, stretch).

## 7. Confound-aware design (inherits the thesis register)

- **CF-2 (chain confound):** every split is **chain-grouped** — a chain never trains and tests together; effective-N is the chain count, not the step count.
- **Anti-collapse circularity:** geometry is read off the **residual only**, never off a regularised latent; Rung 1 (no regulariser) is run first.
- **CF-6 (mean-pooling / sparsity):** trajectories are the sparse 4-behaviour subsequence; every pair carries its **step-gap**, with a `max_gap=1` adjacency control. Mean-pooled local data is *suggestive, not confirmatory* — a last-token / un-pooled re-extraction (GPU) is the confirmatory arm.
- **CF-8 (truncation):** 50% of chains hit the token cap; truncation correlates with category. Correctness labels carry a `truncated` flag and selection prefers complete chains; the contrast must beat a length/gap/truncation baseline.
- **CF-7 (label noise):** correctness comes from an LLM judge (open-ended proofs have no exact-match grader); `confidence` is recorded for a confidence floor, and "uncertain" verdicts are excluded, never coerced.

## 8. Data & current status

- **Model/data:** R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B chains over 1000 tasks × 10 reasoning categories; behaviour-annotated into sentence spans; residual-stream embeddings for 4 behaviours (backtracking, uncertainty-estimation, example-testing, adding-knowledge), mean-pooled, all 28 layers.
- **Built & green:** `src/predict/` (dataset, predictor, evaluation, nulls, labels), unit tests green, real-data plumbing verified (986/1000 chains usable at layer 14, median 20 steps); runners `14_label_correctness.py`–`17_rung2_compare.py`.
- **PILOT EXECUTED (2026-06-15, corrected; `results/predict/R1-1.5B/corrected*/`).** *Label-free:* the linear delta predictor **beats persistence on the full corpus** (residual/persistence 0.88–0.92, displacement R² +0.25–0.31, strongest mid L11–17; the early "worse than persistence" read was a 120-chain small-sample artifact). *Correctness (183 labels = 84✓/99✗):* MODEST — L27 ridge AUC 0.578 (perm p=.034), JEPA 0.591 (p=.024); L14 JEPA 0.582; **L17 dead**. Beats curvature/persistence/length baselines. **Step-shuffle null n.s. (p>0.7) ⇒ the signal is a magnitude/occupancy property, NOT temporal order — H1 supported, H3 unsupported.** JEPA edge small and non-scaling (60-epoch overfit *worsens* forward prediction while AUC holds ⇒ detector value ≠ dynamics skill). Integrity lesson: training the predictor on labelled-only chains inflated AUC to ~0.61 — always train label-agnostic on ALL chains (chain-grouped OOF), evaluate on the labelled subset.
- **Diagnosis (feeds §11):** weak, order-free, dead at the causal layer (L17) and best at the read-out-adjacent layer (L27) — the residual detector reads like an *outcome-proximity echo*, not mechanism; and the field's strong results are outcome-supervised (Sun 0.87 static probe; LRS value-gradient steering), not unsupervised-dynamics residuals.
- **Fork-localization corollary (2026-07-07, `26_fork_locator_validation.py`):** the collapse-ladder E9.3 wanted to use residual spikes OR token entropy as a *fork locator* (inject entropy at branch points). A cheap local gate against annotated branching-behaviour onsets returned **NO-GO**: token entropy scores chain-grouped AUROC 0.624 but **lift@top-20% = 0.77 (<1)** — the highest-entropy tokens are *depleted* of branch points; the residual locator was already H3-null. So the branch is **not** logit-entropy-visible at its onset, coherent with E9.0b (state-before-text: the residual stream commits before the output distribution widens). This is a second, independent confirmation that the unsupervised residual/entropy signal does not localize reasoning branches — the mechanism is upstream of both the residual predictor and the logits. Full note: `RESULTS_LEDGER.md` §B3 + `COLLAPSE_AND_ENTROPY.md` E9.3.

## 9. What counts as success

- **Positive:** Rung-1 (or Rung-2) residual AUC beats Rung-0 curvature, persistence, and length/difficulty, on difficulty-matched chains, with **both nulls rejecting**. → "predictive geometry of reasoning is a correctness signal."
- **Informative null:** Rung-1 ≈ baselines but Rung-2 (nonlinear) clears the bar → "the signal is nonlinear; capacity matters."
- **Hard null:** neither rung beats baselines → a clean, publishable negative that *confirms and extends* SSP (smoothness, static probes, **and** learned-residual geometry all fail) — still a thesis-relevant result about the limits of trajectory geometry.
- **Verdict on rungs 0–2 (2026-07-06):** landed between "positive" and "informative null" — real but weak (0.54–0.59), and the shuffle null reclassifies it as *occupancy*, not dynamics. The operative success criteria for the programme are now §11's sealed per-rung predictions.

## 12. PROPOSED 2026-07-07 — trajectory-dynamics apparatus (A1–A5)

*Not yet sealed. Five additions along the "trajectory of reasoning in latent space" axis, each anchored to JEPA and/or a named mech-interp method, each cheap and each slotting into the R3–R7 ladder without disturbing §11. They are the instruments that let Movement 1 graduate from "reasoning occupies static low-dimensional subspaces" to "**reasoning is a dynamical process with identifiable geometric structure**": discrete modes and switches (A2), an evolving internal belief (A1), failure as collapse into an attractor (A3), mechanised by specific circuit components (A4), over a causally validated forward map (A5). Every item carries a kill criterion; a clean null is reportable. Author-year anchors below are from recall — **verify arXiv ids before thesis inclusion** (§CF-citation discipline).*

**A1 — Belief-state trajectory (logit / tuned lens) = the model's own value track.** `[$0, local]`
- *What.* Read the model's OWN evolving answer-belief instead of (only) training a value head. At each step's read token apply the **logit lens** (unembed the residual) or the **tuned lens** (Belrose et al. 2023 — a learned per-layer affine map, more faithful); track `p_t = P(eventual answer | state_t)` and the lens entropy across the chain. A label-free, un-trained V — the *critic read off the encoder* in the world-model stack (M6).
- *Anchor.* logit lens (nostalgebraist 2020); tuned lens (Belrose et al. 2023); belief-state geometry in the residual stream (Shai et al. 2024).
- *Slot / why.* (i) the strongest label-free baseline R3's trained V must beat — if a free belief-trajectory predicts collapse as well as a GRU, "learned value" is not earned (an honest, load-bearing control). (ii) belief-entropy rise / `p_answer` decay before `detect_loop_tail` = a zero-training R4 precursor. (iii) a 5th M4 frame (the "belief" coordinate). (iv) definitionally order-bearing ⇒ the **M3 positive control the pilot lacked**.
- *Expt.* lens trajectories at L17/L19/L27 over the 986 chains: (a) does belief-entropy lead onset vs the matched-relative-position clean control? (b) chain-grouped AUROC {entropy-slope, `p_answer`} for collapse, paired vs trained V; (c) step-shuffle drop (M3).
- *P.* belief-entropy leads onset by ≥2 sentences above the positional control; trained V beats it by ≤0.05 AUROC. *K.* lens ≥ trained V at all depths ⇒ V redundant ("the model already knows"); report and drop the trained-V arm.

**A2 — Switching linear dynamical system (rSLDS) over the trajectory.** `[$0, local]`
- *What.* Fit `z_{t+1} ≈ A_{k(t)} z_t + b_{k(t)} + ε` with a few discrete modes `k` and learned switching. Modes = reasoning regimes; switch points = candidate branch / backtrack / loop-entry events. Turns the residual-spike hunt into a **generative, order-sensitive dynamical model** (dynamics track, M1).
- *Anchor.* recurrent switching LDS (Linderman et al. 2017; `ssm` library) — the standard apparatus for neural-population trajectories in systems neuroscience.
- *Slot / why.* (i) discrete, interpretable modes+switches = the concrete realization of "dynamical structure" for the thesis. (ii) **unsupervised behaviour recovery** — do inferred modes align with the annotated backtracking/uncertainty/example/knowledge spans? Alignment found WITHOUT labels is a de-confounder for annotator circularity (the deeper risk flagged repo-wide). (iii) a dedicated loop-mode basin = a lead-time precursor (R4).
- *Expt.* fit K=4–8, chain-grouped: (a) mode↔behaviour adjusted-MI vs shuffled-label null; (b) loop-mode entry lead-time vs positional control; (c) switch points vs Rung-1 residual spikes. Control: fit on step-shuffled trajectories (should collapse to ~1 effective mode).
- *P.* mode↔behaviour AMI > null; loop-mode entry leads onset ≥1 sentence; shuffled fit → ~1 mode. *K.* modes neither align with behaviours nor lead onset ⇒ report a null on "discrete reasoning modes."

**A3 — Loops as attractors: the JEPA-collapse ↔ reasoning-collapse bridge.** `[precursor $0 · attractor test ~$2]`
- *What.* JEPA's central failure is **representational collapse** (constant predictor; SIGReg / VICReg / Barlow exist to prevent it). Your anchor failure — loops — IS a **trajectory collapse** (you already measure uniformity@L17 ↑, PR ↓ pre-onset). Unify them: **a loop is an attractor of the latent dynamics, and reasoning-collapse is the dynamical analog of representational-collapse.** This gifts principled collapse metrics as precursors AND a return-after-perturbation causal test.
- *Anchor.* dimensional collapse / effective rank (Jing et al. 2021); SIGReg (LeJEPA); VICReg / Barlow-Twins; fixed-point & attractor analysis of neural dynamics (Sussillo & Barak 2013; Maheswaranathan et al. 2019).
- *Slot / why.* (i) sliding-window effective-rank / SIGReg = new theory-backed R4 precursors. (ii) the **perturbation-return test** is a causal experiment no detector paper (Sun, PHi, LRS) runs. (iii) crystallizes the thesis claim: healthy reasoning = high-dimensional exploratory trajectory; failure = collapse into an attractor (falsifiable).
- *Expt.* (a) sliding-window effective-rank/SIGReg@L17 lead-time vs positional control (→ folds into R4). (b) **attractor test:** at loop onset inject a norm/energy-matched perturbation (random; and anti-loop = −uniformity-axis as contrast), continue generation, measure return-to-loop rate vs perturbation norm = a basin-of-attraction curve. Controls: energy-matched random (M5); clean-chain injection must NOT induce a loop.
- *P.* effective-rank drop leads onset; perturbed loops return below a critical norm (attractor signature); the anti-loop direction escapes = a mechanistically-grounded cheap steering result. *K.* no return structure ⇒ the loop is not an attractor; downgrade to "collapse is a low-D contraction" (still a precursor result).

**A4 — Circuit attribution of trajectory events (induction heads → loops).** `[attribution $0 · ablation ~$1–2]`
- *What.* Everything above is residual-stream geometry (the "where"), not mechanism. Attribute loop onset / residual spikes / mode switches to circuit components. Repetition is classically driven by **induction heads** ([A][B]…[A]→[B]); a loop may BE an induction-head takeover (attention locking to a fixed prior-token offset). Test it — and, crucially, *break* it.
- *Anchor.* induction heads (Olsson et al. 2022); direct logit attribution, activation / path patching, head ablation (Wang et al. IOI 2022; Nanda) — and your own E10.1 interchange discipline.
- *Slot / why.* (i) explains WHY the geometric precursor exists (uniformity collapse ⇐ head H locking to offset-p). (ii) a head-level ablation is a targeted, interpretable alternative knob for R6's gated loop. (iii) the primary **mechanism-not-benchmark** differentiator vs LRS (whose gradient field is never inspected).
- *Expt.* (a) per-head induction scores → candidate loop heads. (b) event-triggered attention-entropy / offset-locking of those heads vs pre-onset (positional control). (c) causal: ablate/patch top-k on collapse-bound prefixes → loop-rate drop (McNemar vs vanilla); DLA of the repeated token to heads. Control: random-head ablation matched on count/norm (M5).
- *P.* ≥1 induction head offset-locks leading onset; top-k ablation breaks a significant loop fraction vs random-head. *K.* no head set leads or breaks loops ⇒ loops are distributed / MLP-driven; report where the mechanism is NOT.

**A5 — Forward-map causal grounding: is the JEPA direction the model's own update?** `[$0–1]`
- *What.* The pilot showed forward-prediction skill and detector value dissociate (overfit worsens `f`, AUC holds). Ground the forward map the way E10.1 grounds the frame: **patch the predicted next-state `ẑ_{t+1}` into the running model** and test whether it yields the model's real continuation.
- *Anchor.* activation patching / interchange interventions (Geiger et al.; Vig et al.) = your E10.1 DAS discipline; the ASM/LRS "steer along the predicted direction" idea, but as VALIDATION not benchmark.
- *Slot / why.* decides whether the dynamics / "trajectory" track is causally earned (M3 tests order; this tests causal fidelity). If `ẑ` patches in cleanly, R7's action-conditioned world-model has a validated substrate; if not, the forward track is diagnostic-only (honest downgrade).
- *Expt.* at step t replace the read-layer residual with `ẑ_{t+1}` (contrast: persistence `z_t`; random matched-norm), teacher-force the real next-step tokens, measure logprob agreement / KL vs unpatched. Agreement(ẑ) > persistence > random validates the map.
- *P.* Agreement(ẑ) > Agreement(persistence), paired-bootstrap CI excluding 0. *K.* ẑ ≤ persistence ⇒ the learned map adds nothing causal over "stay put"; forward track is diagnostic-only, R7 exploratory-only.

**Order-sensitivity ledger (M3).** A1 and A2 are order-sensitive by construction (the pilot's first genuine *dynamics* positives if they fire); A3's attractor test and A5 are causal, not order-statistics; A4 is mechanism. This is deliberate — the pilot's occupancy result (§8) is escaped only by instruments that *need* the arrow of time or a causal intervention.

**Run order & cost.** A1 + A5 ($0, local, fold into R3) → A2 ($0, local) → A4 attribution ($0) / ablation (~$1) → A3 perturbation (~$2, fold into the R4/R6 pod). New pod spend absorbed inside the already-budgeted R4/R6 sessions; total marginal < $5.
