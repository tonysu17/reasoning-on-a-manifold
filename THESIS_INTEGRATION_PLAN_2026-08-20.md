## §A — Presentation doctrine (govern every remaining edit)

1. **Scope, not confidence.** Qualifiers attach to the domain of validity ("at one dose, at
   L17, under greedy decoding…"), never to your belief ("provisional, descriptive…").
2. **Claim sentence, then boundary sentence. Never both in one.** One boundary sentence per
   claim; delete any hedge repeating a hedge in the same paragraph.
3. **Every null is a bound.** Cite its calibration in the same breath (§3.5.3 by reference).
   H2 is the named exception — its floor is declared not-established.
4. **Report the control you ran, not the control you skipped.** "We did not do X" → "we did Y,
   which bounds X."
5. **Disclosures live in the register (§3.6), not at the point of claim.**
6. **Results-first order everywhere:** result → interpretation → limitations. Scope sections
   sit after results sections.
7. **Everything-executed voice (Tony's directive, goes beyond the reframe plan):** no
   "pre-registered but unrun / owed / pending / prospective" anywhere outside §7.4.
   **Registered-family rule:** a registered-but-unexecuted analysis is never silently deleted
   (that misstates the registration = worse science). It gets exactly ONE home — §7.4, framed
   as a falsification test with its sealed design — plus, where a results chapter defines the
   family, a neutral cross-reference ("the pooling/window family is addressed in §7.4"), never
   an inline confession. Provenance/exploratory labels on *executed* work survive (relocated,
   not removed).
8. **Voice anchors:** "graded, not uneven — grading it is the contribution." The register the
   whole thesis should be in: *"Common-L27 correlation-dimension estimates are 7.04–8.25, but
   within-chain label shuffles are equally low or lower."* Hedge-pass search list: descriptive,
   provisional, bounded, amended, only, merely, does not establish, narrows.
9. **Every new number enters the results register** and the Week-4 37-quantity consistency
   pass extends to cover it. Numbers live in ONE location; other chapters reference.
